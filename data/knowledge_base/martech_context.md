# Martech Context

## Marketing Technology

Important marketing technology areas include:

- CRM
- Customer Data Platforms
- Marketing automation
- Marketing analytics
- Advertising technology
- Personalization platforms
- Customer engagement platforms

## Technology Evaluation

Marketing technology should be evaluated based on:

- business value
- integration capability
- scalability
- data quality
- customer experience
- operational efficiency
- security and governance

## Martech Trends

Changes in advertising platforms, AI capabilities,
analytics systems, and customer data infrastructure
may affect marketing operations and campaign strategy.
