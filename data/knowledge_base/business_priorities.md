# Business Priorities

## Priority Areas

The organization prioritizes:

1. Customer acquisition
2. Customer experience
3. Marketing efficiency
4. AI adoption
5. Martech modernization
6. Data-driven decision making

## Decision Making

Marketing intelligence should help leaders understand:

- what changed
- why the change matters
- potential business impact
- whether action may be required

## Competitive Awareness

Major competitor campaigns, product launches,
partnerships, acquisitions, and technology changes
should be monitored for potential strategic implications.
