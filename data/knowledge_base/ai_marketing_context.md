# AI in Marketing Context

## Key Use Cases

AI can support:

- content generation
- campaign optimization
- audience segmentation
- personalization
- predictive analytics
- customer service
- marketing automation

## Business Considerations

AI initiatives should be evaluated based on:

- measurable business value
- accuracy
- data quality
- customer trust
- transparency
- governance
- operational impact

## Strategic Impact

Major developments from AI platforms may influence:

- advertising strategy
- customer acquisition
- personalization
- marketing productivity
- competitive positioning
- marketing technology investments
