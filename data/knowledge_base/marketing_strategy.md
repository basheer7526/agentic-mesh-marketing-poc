# Marketing Strategy

## Strategic Focus

The organization focuses on measurable marketing growth through
customer acquisition, customer engagement, personalization,
and efficient use of marketing technology.

## Customer Acquisition

Key acquisition channels include digital advertising,
content marketing, search, social media, and targeted campaigns.

Marketing initiatives should be evaluated based on their
potential effect on lead generation, conversion, and customer growth.

## Customer Experience

The organization values personalized and consistent customer
experiences across digital channels.

Personalization, customer engagement, retention, and loyalty
are important strategic considerations.

## AI Adoption

AI should be evaluated for practical marketing use cases such as:

- campaign optimization
- content generation
- personalization
- customer segmentation
- marketing analytics
- customer engagement

AI adoption should consider data quality, governance,
customer trust, and measurable business value.
